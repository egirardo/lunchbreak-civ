# How to Play — Lunchbreak Civ

> A quick-reference guide for players. Read this in ~2 minutes.

## The Goal

Build the best civilization in **30 turns** (~20 minutes). You win by having the highest **Score** at the end, or by achieving a special victory (conquest, science, or culture).

---

## The Basics

### Each Turn, You Can:

1. **Move your units** to explore, gather resources, or attack enemies
2. **Build** units or buildings in your cities
3. **Research** a tech to unlock new units and buildings
4. **Manage your cities** — choose what they focus on (food, production, or science)

Then **End Turn**. Your opponents move, and the turn counter goes up.

---

## Units

You start with a **Settler** (founds cities) and a **Warrior** (attacks enemies).

| Unit | What It Does | Cost | Special |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Settler** | Founds a new city (consumed) | 20 prod | Moves 2 tiles/turn |
| **Warrior** | Attacks enemies | 20 prod | Strength: 5, Moves 2 tiles/turn |
| **Archer** | Attacks from 2 tiles away | 20 prod | Strength: 7, Ranged attacker (Unlocked: Archery) |
| **Horseman** | Fast attacker | 25 prod | Strength: 8, Moves 4 tiles/turn (Unlocked: Horseback Riding) |
| **Swordsman** | Strong warrior upgrade | — | Strength: 12 (Warriors auto-upgrade with Iron Working) |
| **Worker** | Builds Farms (+1 food) and Mines (+1 production) on tiles | 15 prod | Moves 2 tiles/turn, no combat, takes 1 turn per improvement |

## Cities

Cities are the heart of your civilization. Each city:

- **Produces resources** from the tiles around it:
  - 🌾 **Food** → grows your population
  - ⚙️ **Production** → builds units and buildings
  - 🔬 **Science** → unlocks new technologies
- **Builds one item at a time** (a unit or a building)
- **Has a limit**: you can build 4 cities (5 with Civil Service tech)
- **Claims territory**: the tiles within 1 tile of the city (2 tiles once it reaches 4 population)
- **Makes science on its own**: 1 science + 1 per 2 population, plus building bonuses

### Growing a City

Food accumulates in a city. When it reaches **10 food**, you gain 1 population and the counter resets. More population = more tiles to work = more resources. Each city can grow up to **6 population** max.

### Focusing a City

Click a city to set its **focus**:
- **Food Focus**: prioritize food tiles (grow population faster)
- **Production Focus**: prioritize production tiles (build units/buildings faster)
- **Science Focus**: +50% science in this city (research techs faster) — only works if the city has a Library

---

## Technology

**Research** one tech at a time. As your cities produce science, it fills a progress bar. When the bar is full, you unlock that tech and get access to new units, buildings, or bonuses.

There are **12 techs** in three branches:

- **Economy**: farms, mines, workshops, more cities
- **Military**: walls, archers, horsemen, strong units
- **Science & Culture**: libraries, temples, universities, research bonuses

You can't research all 12 in 30 turns — choose wisely.

---

## Combat

Attack an enemy unit or city by moving your unit onto theirs (melee) or attacking from 2 tiles away (ranged).

**How It Works:**
- Attacker's **strength** vs Defender's **strength**. Whoever is stronger wins **instantly**.
- If strengths are tied, the defender wins and the attacker is removed.
- **Flanking**: you get **+1 strength** if another of your units is next to the target.
- The loser is removed from the board. Combat is quick — no damage over multiple rounds.
- **Archers** can attack from 2 tiles away without moving into melee. If an Archer's ranged attack fails, nothing happens and the Archer survives. Other units must be adjacent.

**Attacking and Defending Cities:**
- A city defends with strength **5**, or with its garrison unit's strength if that is higher.
- **Walls** give a **+50% defense bonus** (e.g. 5 becomes 7.5 → 7).
- Win the attack and you **capture** the city, buildings included.
- Capture an enemy's **capital** and that player is eliminated. Lose your own capital and you're out!

**Tips:**
- Build stronger units (Swordsmen: 12 vs Warriors: 5 is a huge jump)
- Use Archers to poke enemies from a distance without risk
- Attack isolated enemies; avoid attacking a city with Walls unless your strength is much higher
- Horsemen are fast and strong — great for hit-and-run tactics

## Winning

### Score Victory (Default)

The player with the **highest score** wins at turn 30.

You earn points from:
| Source | Points |
|---|---|
| Each city | 10 points (one-time) |
| Each population | 1 point per pop (counted at the end) |
| Each tech researched | 5 points per tech |
| Each tile you control | 1 point per tile |
| Each Temple | 2 points per turn (keep building them!) |
| Each University | 3 points per turn |

**Tiebreaker:** If players are tied at turn 30, whoever has more techs wins. If still tied, whoever has more cities wins.

### Instant Wins

- **Domination**: Capture all enemy capitals. Move a unit onto an enemy's capital city to capture it instantly and eliminate them. First to capture all enemies wins.
- **Science**: Research all 12 techs. Hard to do in 30 turns, but rewarding if you pull it off.
- **Culture**: Accumulate 25 Culture points. Temples give 2 Culture per turn (one per city). Rush Writing → Mathematics → Philosophy and build Temples early to pull it off before turn 30.

## Tips for New Players

1. **Expand early**: settle a 2nd city before turn 10 to boost your resource production
2. **Balance**: don't ignore military — neighbors will attack if you're weak
3. **Tech wisely**: pick techs that unlock units/buildings you'll actually build
4. **Watch the timer**: you only have ~20 minutes, so don't spend too long per turn
5. **Pause when needed**: the game auto-saves every turn, so you can come back to it

## Controls

- **Click a tile** to move a unit, build a city, or gather resources
- **Click a city** to build something or change focus
- **Press Space or Enter** to end your turn
- **Hover over things** to see tooltips with more info
- **Press ? to open this guide in-game**
